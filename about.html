Our Story

22 Celebration Co. was created to transform the gift-giving experience. No stress. No confusion. No last-minute rush. Just beautiful curated gift boxes and fully customized baskets for any of the 22 major holidays — and the special moments in between.

Why We Exist

Everyone loves receiving a thoughtful gift — but finding the right one can be overwhelming. Our mission is to make gift shopping:

  • ✨ Easy
  • 🎁 Personal
  • ⏱️ Quick
  • 💙 Meaningful

Our Products

We offer two main types of products:

  • Pre-Packaged Gift Boxes: Themed for each holiday or occasion.
  • Custom Gift Baskets: Fully personalized to match interests, hobbies, sports teams, colors, personalities, and more.

How Custom Pricing Works

Custom baskets are priced at approximately 2× the item value. Example: If a customer wants a $40 Carolina Panthers themed birthday basket, the price would be $80 including customization and assembly.

Our Commitment

Whether it’s Christmas, a birthday, graduation, or a custom themed order, we ensure quality, creativity, and care in every package.
