About 22 Celebration Co.

22 Celebration Co. was created with one simple idea: make gift giving easier, more thoughtful, and more fun for everyone.

Our Theme: Gifts for 22 Major Holidays & More

We specialize in theme-oriented gift boxes and baskets covering 22 major holidays and the most important life moments in between – birthdays, weddings, baby showers, graduations, retirements and more.

Every box and basket is designed around the person receiving it: their age, style, interests and the occasion you’re celebrating.

Our Objective: Take the Stress Out of Shopping

Instead of spending hours searching for the “perfect” present, you tell us who it’s for and what you want to spend. We handle the curation, packaging and presentation.

Whether you’re buying one gift or coordinating matching gifts for a whole family, 22 Celebration Co. keeps it simple and beautiful.

What We Offer

Gift Boxes

Sleek, curated boxes themed for specific holidays and occasions. Ideal for quick, premium-feeling gifts at a set price.

Gift Baskets

Larger, more detailed presentations that can include food, clothing, self-care items, fan gear, perfume and more.

Custom Experiences

Want a Carolina Panthers birthday basket with specific items and a set budget? We’ll create a custom themed basket around your idea.

How Custom Pricing Works

Custom gift boxes & baskets are priced at a premium compared to standard boxes. As an example:

  • $40 worth of Panthers gifts in a basket → you pay around $80.
  • This covers planning, sourcing, packaging and presentation.
  • You get a one-of-a-kind gift that feels personal and memorable.
